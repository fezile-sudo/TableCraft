Our Story

Where tradition meets imagination

We don't want to
forget where food comes from.

TableCraft is a modern African dining experience inspired by the people, ingredients and food traditions of Southern Africa.

We take familiar flavours and give them room to evolve — without losing the stories behind them.

Our beginning

Born from a love
of the Eastern Cape.

TableCraft began as a simple idea between friends who shared a belief: African food deserves a place at the centre of the modern dining conversation.

What started as experimental dinners grew into a restaurant concept built around fire, spice, seasonal ingredients and the memories that come with food.

Our kitchen draws inspiration from the Eastern Cape and the wider Southern African region, while remaining curious about flavours from everywhere.
